Data-center electricity payments

The Cloud Dividend redirects part of the existing consumption tax. At the same usage and tariff, it adds $0 to a data center’s present bill while that tax is in effect.

Current individual data-center bills and a verified statewide electricity-spending total are not available in the project data. The example below uses editable assumptions, not reported current payments. Enter an actual effective utility rate to estimate a particular facility.

Definitions and scenario assumptions

Energy burden is total energy cost divided by total nonnegative income, weighted by household counts. The affordability gap sums each cell’s cost above 6% of income. Household counts use cell-average burdens, not individual household records. Reference income and nonelectric costs follow the notebook’s 2022–2026 adjustment; the electricity price is $0.1683/kWh.

The surcharge is the monthly increase for a typical 1,000-kWh customer; each cell’s cost changes by its annual kWh × surcharge / 1,000. Income change applies to all households relative to the 2026 reference. Savings reduce low-income energy costs by the selected percentage. Annual relief is a hypothetical equal payment to every low-income household, capped at its remaining energy cost. It is not an estimate of EAP reach, eligibility, or delivery. Relief is treated as a reduction in out-of-pocket cost. Savings, income change, and relief are user assumptions; presets reproduce the notebook’s price-only scenarios, not forecasts.
